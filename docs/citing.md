# Citing frost

A paper describing frost is in preparation. Until it is available, please
cite the software. The repository's
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/pravirkr/frost/blob/main/CITATION.cff)
gives the details; GitHub's "Cite this repository" button formats them.

Please also cite the methods frost relies on:

- **FDMT**: Zackay, B. & Ofek, E. O. (2017), *An Accurate and Efficient
  Algorithm for Detection of Radio Bursts with an Unknown Dispersion
  Measure, for Single-Dish Telescopes and Interferometers*,
  [ApJ 835, 11](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/835/1/11).
- **dmt**, the dedispersion library frost uses:
  [github.com/pravirkr/dmt](https://github.com/pravirkr/dmt).

and, for the RFI strategies you enable:

- **IQRM**: Morello, V. et al. (2022).
- **Kadane searches**: Men, Y. & Barr, E. (2024), A&A 683, A183.
- **zdot**: Men, Y. et al. (2019).
- **Zero-DM filtering**: Eatough, R. P., Keane, E. F. & Lyne, A. G. (2009).
